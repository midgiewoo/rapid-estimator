A LEGACY OF PRECISION

Four decades building the tools America’s builders trust.

Rapid Estimator software modules are products of SDSI Business Systems, Inc. Since its inception in 1984, SDSI has been producing innovative estimating software for small-to-medium sized construction companies. In addition to estimating software, SDSI develops custom applications and offers remote system support to clients on demand.

SINCE 1984

The SDSI Story

SDSI Business Systems was founded in Indianapolis by a small group of engineers and former construction professionals who saw a critical gap: estimating software that was either too simplistic or overwhelmingly complex and expensive.

From the very beginning, we committed to three principles that still guide every decision we make:

  • 1. Build software that estimators actually love to use.
  • 2. Never sacrifice accuracy for speed — or the reverse.
  • 3. Stand behind every customer with real, responsive human support.

Over 40 years later, we remain an independent, privately held company. Our flagship product, Rapid Estimator, is used daily by more than 1,200 contractors across the United States and Canada. We’ve also built hundreds of custom Windows applications for clients who needed specialized tools that off-the-shelf software could never provide.
