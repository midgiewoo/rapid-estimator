Smart Assemblies

Create reusable assemblies that expand into dozens of line items with correct labor productivity, crew composition, and material pricing. Update once, apply everywhere.

Bid & Change Management

Version control, alternate pricing, unit price breakdowns, and complete change order tracking. Know exactly what changed between revisions.

Professional Proposals

Quickly produce reports to match any level of detail. Win/loss analysis, category breakdowns, labor vs material ratios, historical comparison, and custom reports. Data you can actually use to improve future bids and maintain profit.
